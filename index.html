Delivery methodology

A repeatable path from
request to closeout.

The exact controls vary by risk and complexity, but the operating sequence remains consistent.

Intake

Confirm objective, expected output, deadline and relevant information.

Scope

Define deliverables, roles, commercial basis and approval requirements.

Risk & confidentiality

Assess information sensitivity, access, third parties and specialist requirements.

Plan & assign

Allocate resources, responsibilities, timeline and production approach.

Produce & execute

Complete the work under defined controls and communication points.

Quality review

Check completeness, accuracy, formatting, rights, evidence and requirements.

Client approval

Deliver for review, capture feedback and control revisions.

Closeout

Reconcile deliverables, evidence, payment and final files.

Improve

Capture lessons learned and retain or delete records as required.
